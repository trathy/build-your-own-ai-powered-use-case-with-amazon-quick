Dashboards bring visual analytics into Quick with interactive charts and a natural language Q&A bar.

## Step 1: Create a Dashboard

1. Navigate to **Dashboards** in Amazon Quick
2. Create a new dashboard from your data

## Step 2: Prepare golden questions

Ask Quick to suggest questions:

```text
Based on my dataset, suggest 5 questions I could ask the Dashboard
Q&A bar that would produce clear, useful visuals. Make them specific
to the metrics and entities in my data.
```

## Step 3: Test each question

For every question, verify:
- [ ] The visual type matches the question (trend = line chart, ranking = bar chart)
- [ ] Data values match your dataset
- [ ] Response time is acceptable

## Step 4: Practice the drill-down

1. Start with an overview visual
2. Click to filter on the worst-performing entity
3. Drill from summary to detail
4. Reset to the overview

> **Tip:** Practice this flow until it is smooth. Know exactly which clicks take you from the big picture to the root cause.

## What makes this powerful

> "Type a question, get a chart. Click a bar, the whole dashboard filters. No BI training needed. Just ask."
