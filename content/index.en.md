# Build Your Own AI-Powered Use Case with Amazon Quick

### Define a use case. Generate the data. Build with agentic AI and analytics. See it in action.

In this workshop you will start from a blank slate, define a business use case relevant to your organization, use Amazon Quick to generate realistic synthetic data, then build a complete AI-powered experience using Quick's agentic and analytics capabilities.

There is no fixed scenario. You choose the industry, the personas, and the business questions. Quick does the heavy lifting.

> **Note:** This workshop uses synthetic data only. No real company data is required or used at any point.

**Duration:** Approximately 90 minutes

**No coding required.**
