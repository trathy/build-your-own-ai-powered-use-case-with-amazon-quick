## Step 1: Choose your focus area

Pick the area of your business where AI-powered insights would have the most impact. Here are common starting points:

| Focus area | Example questions | Common personas |
|------------|------------------|----------------|
| **Supply chain** | On-time delivery, inventory levels, supplier reliability | Supply chain director, logistics manager, procurement lead |
| **Sales & Revenue** | Pipeline health, conversion rates, account performance | Sales VP, account manager, revenue analyst |
| **Operations** | Productivity, quality, throughput, maintenance | VP operations, plant manager, maintenance director |
| **Finance** | Margin analysis, cost trends, budget variance | CFO, controller, FP&A analyst |
| **Marketing** | Campaign ROI, customer acquisition cost, channel performance | CMO, demand gen manager, marketing analyst |
| **HR / People** | Attrition, hiring velocity, engagement scores | CHRO, talent acquisition lead, HR business partner |
| **Customer Success** | Churn risk, NPS, support ticket resolution | Customer success VP, support manager, account manager |

## Step 2: Define 2-3 personas

Who in your organization would use Quick? Pick roles that touch the same data but ask different questions. The cross-functional perspective is what makes the demo powerful.

**Template:**
```
Persona 1: _______________ (role, what they care about most)
Persona 2: _______________ (role, what they care about most)
Persona 3: _______________ (role, what they care about most)
```

**Example:**
```
Persona 1: VP Supply Chain (on-time delivery, inventory health, supplier risk)
Persona 2: Demand Planner (forecast accuracy, seasonal patterns, new launches)
Persona 3: Category Manager (promotion effectiveness, margin, assortment)
```

> **Tip:** Choose personas from different teams that share the same underlying data. The strongest demo moment is when Quick connects insights across those teams.
