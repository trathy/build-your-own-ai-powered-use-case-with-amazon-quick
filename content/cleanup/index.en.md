Remove all workshop resources to keep your environment clean.

## Step 1: Delete the Quick App

1. Navigate to **Apps**
2. Find the app you created
3. Delete it

## Step 2: Delete the Topic

1. Navigate to **Topics**
2. Find your Topic
3. Delete it

## Step 3: Delete the Flow

1. Navigate to **Flows**
2. Find your Flow
3. Delete it

## Step 4: Delete Chat Agents

1. Navigate to **Chat Agents**
2. Delete each agent you created

## Step 5: Delete the Space

1. Navigate to **Spaces**
2. Find your Space
3. Delete it (this also removes the uploaded files)

## Step 6: Delete the Dashboard

1. Navigate to **Dashboards**
2. Delete any dashboards you created

## Verify cleanup

Confirm that none of your workshop resources appear in Spaces, Apps, Topics, Flows, Dashboards, or Chat Agents.

> **Tip:** If you want to recreate this later, save your prompts and data files locally. You can rebuild the entire environment in about 15 minutes by re-running the prompts from Modules 2-4.
