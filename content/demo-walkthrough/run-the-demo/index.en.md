## The eight-beat arc

| # | Beat | What you show | Time |
|---|------|--------------|------|
| 1 | **Start with the problem** | Name the business challenge. Do not mention Quick yet. | 2 min |
| 2 | **The shared brain** | Space: cross-file query that reasons across sources | 3 min |
| 3 | **Role-based intelligence** | Chat Agents: two personas answer the same question differently | 3 min |
| 4 | **Deep analysis** | Research: show the structured root-cause briefing | 2 min |
| 5 | **Visual exploration** | Dashboard: show a chart, then use the Q&A bar | 3 min |
| 6 | **Trusted numbers** | Topic: same metric asked two ways, show lineage | 2 min |
| 7 | **Insight to action** | Quick App: open the scorecard, show risk alerts | 2 min |
| 8 | **Your turn** | Ask your own question against the data | 5 min |

**Target: 22 minutes.** Drop beat 4 or 6 if you need to shorten.

## Opening frame

> "Our team spends hours pulling data from multiple systems, cross-referencing in spreadsheets, and writing summaries that are outdated by the time they reach leadership. Everyone touches the same data but nobody has the same view. What if that fragmentation just went away?"

## Transitions between beats

- **Space to Chat Agent:** "That was one answer. But different roles need different lenses. Watch."
- **Chat Agent to Research:** "Quick doesn't just answer. It can do a full deep-dive."
- **Research to Dashboard:** "Some people want text. Others want charts. Same data, different view."
- **Dashboard to Topic:** "That chart is useful, but how do I know the number is right?"
- **Topic to Quick App:** "Now imagine the insight is always on, packaged into a tool."
