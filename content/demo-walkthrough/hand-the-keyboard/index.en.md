## Try your own questions

Now it is your turn. Use any of the capabilities you built:

- Ask the **Space** a question you did not plan for
- Switch to a **Chat Agent** and see how it responds differently
- Open the **Dashboard** Q&A bar and type a new question
- Query the **Topic** and check the data lineage
- Use the **Quick App** to explore a different entity

## Suggested exploration questions

Try these patterns:

1. **What-if:** "What happens if {entity} improves by {X}%?"
2. **Comparison:** "Compare {entity A} and {entity B} on {metric}"
3. **Trend:** "Show me the {metric} trend for {entity} over the last 12 months"
4. **Root cause:** "Why is {metric} declining at {entity}?"
5. **Action:** "Draft a {document type} summarizing the {situation}"
6. **The gap:** "What is something we cannot answer with this data?" (tests the deliberate gap)

## Reflect

Consider these questions:

- Which capability was most valuable for your use case?
- Which persona would benefit most from having this on day one?
- What real data sources would you connect to replicate this with live data?
- What workflows could you automate with Flows?
