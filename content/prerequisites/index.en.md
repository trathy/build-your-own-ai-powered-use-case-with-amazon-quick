Complete these before the workshop begins.

## 1. Amazon Quick access

You need access to Amazon Quick on the web. Sign in at your organization's Amazon Quick URL.

If you do not have access yet, contact your AWS administrator or request access through your organization's standard provisioning process.

## 2. Supported browser

Chrome, Edge, or Firefox. No extensions or plugins required.

## 3. Think about your use case

Before the workshop, think about what you want to build. You will define this fully in Module 1. Come prepared with:

- The **area of your business** you want to explore (e.g., supply chain, sales, operations, finance, HR)
- A **persona** who would benefit from AI-powered insights (e.g., VP of Operations, demand planner, risk analyst)
- A **business question** that is hard to answer today (e.g., "Why are we missing delivery targets?" or "Which campaigns are underperforming?")

> **Tip:** The more specific you are, the more realistic your results will be. "Supply chain" is okay. "On-time delivery performance across our top 5 distribution centers" is much better.
