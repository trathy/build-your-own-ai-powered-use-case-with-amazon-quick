A Space is a shared knowledge base that indexes your files for natural language querying. Anyone with access can ask questions and get answers grounded in the actual data.

## Step 1: Create a Space

1. In Amazon Quick, navigate to **Spaces**
2. Click **Create Space**
3. Name it descriptively (e.g., "{Your Focus Area} Intelligence Hub")
4. Add a description of what data is inside

## Step 2: Upload your data

Upload all CSV files and the situation brief (if you created one) from your local folder. Wait for indexing to complete.

## Step 3: Test the Space

Run these queries in order, from simple to complex:

**Simple lookup:**
```text
What is the current {central metric} for {worst-performing entity}?
```

**Comparison:**
```text
Rank all {entities} by {central metric}. Show the trend direction.
```

**Cross-file reasoning:**
```text
Why is {worst entity} underperforming? Look across all the data.
```

**Recommendation:**
```text
What are the top 3 actions to improve {central metric} for {worst entity}?
Ground your recommendations in the data.
```

## What makes this powerful

> "I uploaded the data once. Now anyone on the team can ask questions in plain language and get answers grounded in the actual files. No SQL, no BI training, no waiting for an analyst."
