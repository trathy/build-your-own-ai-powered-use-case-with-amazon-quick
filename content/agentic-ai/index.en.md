In this module you will use Quick's agentic AI capabilities to turn your synthetic data into an intelligent, queryable experience.

You will build four things:
1. A **Space** -- shared knowledge base
2. **Chat Agents** -- persona-based assistants
3. A **Research** deep-dive -- structured analysis
4. A **Flow** -- automated workflow

**The story:** "Quick does not just answer questions. It understands context, adapts to different roles, conducts research, and automates workflows."
