Research is Quick's deep-dive capability. It conducts multi-source analysis, synthesizes findings, and produces structured deliverables with citations.

## Step 1: Run a Research task

```text
Research the following question using all available data in the
"{Space name}" Space:

{Your H2 question -- the root cause question}

Produce a structured analysis with:
1. Executive summary (3-4 sentences)
2. Root cause breakdown with supporting data
3. Timeline of how the problem developed
4. Impact quantification
5. Recommended actions with owners and priorities
6. Sources cited for every claim
```

## Step 2: Review the output

Verify:
- [ ] Cites specific data points from your files
- [ ] Follows a clear, logical structure
- [ ] Distinguishes between what the data shows and what it implies
- [ ] Includes actionable recommendations

## What makes this powerful

> "I asked one question. Quick searched across all the data, identified the root cause, built a timeline, quantified the impact, and produced a structured briefing with sources. That is an analyst's work in seconds."
