## What is Amazon Quick?

Amazon Quick is an AI-powered work companion that connects to your files, communications, and data sources. It builds an understanding of your work context that deepens over time, turning questions into answers, answers into actions, and actions into outcomes for you and your entire team.

## What you will build

By the end of this workshop, you will have:

1. A **defined use case** tailored to your industry and role
2. A **synthetic dataset** generated by Quick that supports a realistic story
3. **Agentic AI capabilities** configured for your use case: a Space (shared knowledge base), Chat Agents (persona-based assistants), Research (deep-dive analysis), and a Flow (automated workflow)
4. **Analytics capabilities** configured for your use case: a Dashboard (visual analytics with natural language Q&A), a Topic (governed metrics with data lineage), and a Quick App (interactive tool)
5. A **rehearsed walkthrough** you can run in 20-25 minutes

## Capabilities covered

### Agentic AI

| Capability | What it does |
|------------|-------------|
| **Spaces** | Shared knowledge bases the team queries in plain language |
| **Chat Agents** | Persona-based AI assistants with custom instructions and data access |
| **Research** | Multi-source deep-dive analysis with citations and structured output |
| **Flows** | Automated multi-step workflows triggered by events or on demand |

### Analytics

| Capability | What it does |
|------------|-------------|
| **Dashboards** | Visual analytics with interactive charts and a natural language Q&A bar |
| **Topics** | Governed data domains with defined business terms, access controls, and data lineage |
| **Quick Apps** | Interactive web applications built conversationally from your data |

## Workshop structure

| Module | What you do | Time |
|--------|------------|------|
| Prerequisites | Confirm access and setup | 5 min |
| Module 1: Define Your Use Case | Pick industry, personas, and business questions | 10 min |
| Module 2: Generate Synthetic Data | Use Quick to create a realistic dataset | 15 min |
| Module 3: Agentic AI | Build Spaces, Chat Agents, Research, and Flows | 20 min |
| Module 4: Analytics | Build Dashboards, Topics, and Quick Apps | 20 min |
| Module 5: Walkthrough | Run the full experience end-to-end | 15 min |
| Cleanup | Remove all workshop resources | 5 min |

**Total: approximately 90 minutes**
